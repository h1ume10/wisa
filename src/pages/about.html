About WISA

A Firm Built By The People Who Used To Decide These Cases

WISA was founded by attorneys, labour law and labour relations practitioners, and former Bargaining Council and CCMA Commissioners, brought together around one goal: getting workplace investigations right.

Who We Are

WISA is a professional services firm offering advisory, consulting, and training to organisations conducting workplace investigations. We are dedicated to promoting fair procedure fact-finding processes in investigating alleged workplace misconduct, in compliance with the Labour Relations Act and the Constitution of the Republic of South Africa.

Our objective is to standardise and professionalise the conduct of workplace investigations in South Africa, to the level of global best practice, working across both the private sector and the public service.

Who We Serve

  • HR and ER practitioners
  • In-house attorneys
  • Compliance officials
  • Internal investigators
  • Security managers
  • Trade union practitioners

Our Work Ethic And Philosophy

We see ourselves as a foremost authority and thought leader in workplace investigation. Rather than a loose collection of individual consultants, we are working to build a genuine community of practitioners: people who call themselves Workplace Investigators in South Africa, and who hold each other to the same standard.
